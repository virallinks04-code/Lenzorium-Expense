## Full description (max 4000)
Lenzorium Expenses answers three questions every day: where did my money go, how much do I owe, and how much do I have left?

QUICK DAILY TRACKING
• Add an expense in seconds — just the amount and a category
• Note the merchant, payment method (cash, bank or card), date and time
• Attach a receipt photo
• Record income from salary, business, freelance work, gifts and more
• Create your own categories with custom icons and colours

YOUR BALANCE, ALWAYS ACCURATE
• Available balance updates the moment you save
• See today's and this month's spending and income at a glance
• Spending breakdown by category
• Optional "balance after clearing debt" view
• Warnings before your balance goes negative

LOANS AND REPAYMENTS, KEPT SEPARATE
• Track money you've borrowed, from whom, and when it's due
• Record partial or full repayments
• Clear status for every loan: Active, Partially repaid or Repaid
• Overdue alerts for missed due dates
• Borrowed money is never counted as income, and repayments are never counted as everyday expenses

CLEAR REPORTS
• Daily, weekly, monthly and custom date ranges
• Opening and closing balance for every period
• Charts for spending by category, income vs expenses and spending trends
• Debt summary by lender
• Export reports as PDF or CSV

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works completely offline — no account, no sign-up
• No ads, no tracking, no internet access
• Optional PIN and fingerprint/face lock
• Full backup and restore, including receipt photos
• Included in your phone's encrypted Google backup when Android backup is on

Light and dark themes. One currency of your choice; no conversion.
