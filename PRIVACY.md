# Privacy Policy — Lenzorium Expenses

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Lenzorium Expenses is a personal expense, income and loan tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

Lenzorium Expenses keeps your financial records **on your phone**. The app has no account, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. It does not request internet access, and it never sends your data to the developer or to anyone else.

## Information the app stores

- The transactions, income, loans, repayments, categories and notes you enter
- Receipt photos you choose to attach
- Budgets, bills and regular income, savings goals, shortcuts and tags you create
- Your settings (currency, theme, and an encrypted hash of your PIN if you set one)
- If you turn on password-protected automatic backups, that password, encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore)

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

## Google backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google account backup so it can be restored when you set up a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and Google, is encrypted, and is not accessible to the developer. You can turn it off in **Android Settings › Google › Backup**.

## Files you create

Backup files and PDF/CSV reports are created only when you tap the corresponding button, or, if you turn on automatic backup, on the schedule you choose, in the folder you choose. That folder can belong to a cloud storage app such as Google Drive; the upload is then done by that app under its own terms, not by Lenzorium. You can protect backups with a password (AES-256 encryption); without the password nobody, including the developer, can open them. Keep unprotected backup files private, because they contain your full records.

## Permissions

- **Biometrics** (optional): to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face. Biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware and is never seen by the app.
- **Notifications** (optional, Android 13+ asks you): bill reminders and budget warnings. Notifications never show amounts, and on the lock screen they show only a generic line.
- **Run at startup / wake lock**: used by Android's job scheduler to run the once-a-day check for due bills, budget warnings and automatic backup.
- **Camera**: the app does not hold the camera permission. When you choose to photograph a receipt, your phone's camera app takes the picture and hands it to Lenzorium.

## Receipt reading

"Read amount from receipt" uses Google ML Kit text recognition with the model built into the app. The photo is processed on your phone and is never uploaded. The app does not have internet access, so ML Kit's optional usage logging cannot send anything.

## Data sharing

The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal or financial data.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone. Backup files you saved yourself must be deleted separately, and Google backups can be managed in your Google account.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app.
