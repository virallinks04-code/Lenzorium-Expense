# Lenzorium Expenses — User Guide

## First launch

1. **Currency** — choose the one currency your ledger uses. Nothing is converted.
2. **Current balance** — the money you have available right now (cash + bank). Don't subtract what you owe.
3. **Start date** — the day your balance applies from. Entries can't be dated earlier.
4. **Existing loans** (optional) — money you already owe. These count as debt but are *not* added to your balance again.
5. **PIN lock** (optional) — a 4–8 digit PIN, plus fingerprint/face unlock if your phone supports it.

Already have a backup? Tap **I have a backup — restore it** on the welcome screen.

## Home

- **Available balance · now** — what you have today.
- **Outstanding debt** — what you still owe across all loans (tap to open Loans).
- **After clearing debt** — balance minus debt (can be hidden in Settings).
- **This month** — today's expenses, this month's expenses and income, and loan repayments (shown separately — they are not expenses).
- Spending by category, upcoming loan due dates, and recent transactions.

## Recording money

Tap **Add Expense** on Home, or the **Add** tab for all four options.

- **Expense** — amount and category are all you need. Date/time defaults to now and payment method to Cash. Optionally add the merchant, notes and a receipt photo.
- **Income** — amount, category, and optionally the source.
- **Take loan** — lender, amount received, date, and an optional due date. It increases both your balance and your debt. It is not income.
- **Repay loan** — choose the loan and the amount (**Pay in full** fills the remaining amount). Repayments lower your balance and your debt. You can't repay more than is owed.

If saving would make your balance negative, the app asks you to confirm first.

## Transactions

Everything in date order. Search by merchant, source, lender or notes, and filter by type, category, payment method or dates. Tap an entry to see details, edit, or delete it. Balances update immediately.

A loan's *money received* entry can only be removed by deleting the loan itself (the app explains this), so loan history isn't lost by accident.

## Loans

Open from **Loans** on Home or Transactions. Each loan shows borrowed, repaid and outstanding amounts, its status (**Active**, **Partially repaid**, **Repaid**), an **Overdue** warning after the due date, and its full history. On a loan's page you can repay, add a fee/interest charge (adds to debt only), edit, or delete it.

## Reports

Choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **Custom**, and use the arrows to move between periods. You'll see total income and expenses, the opening and closing balance for the period, spending by category, income vs expenses, a spending trend, borrowing and repayments, and debt by lender (as of the period's end and current).

**Export** the report as PDF or CSV. Exports are for reading and sharing — they are **not backups**.

## Settings

- Currency, opening balance, tracking start date, categories (add/rename/delete — deleted categories' entries move to *Other*).
- Theme: system, light or dark. Show/hide *Balance after clearing debt*.
- PIN lock and fingerprint/face unlock. The app re-locks after 30 seconds in the background.
- **Create full backup** — one .zip file with all transactions, loans, categories, settings and receipt photos. Save it somewhere off the phone (e.g. Google Drive).
- **Restore from backup** — replaces the data on this phone. The backup is fully checked first; if it can't be read, your current data is left untouched.

## Good to know

- Cash, Bank and Card are labels for how you paid. They are not separate account balances, and credit-card limits or card debt are not tracked.
- Everything is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it — make a backup first.
